# Quiz Rules

Please read these rules carefully before you begin. Once you select "I have read and agree — start the quiz", the quiz opens in fullscreen and your environment is monitored throughout.

## Environment requirements

- The screen switches to **fullscreen** when the quiz starts. Please do not leave fullscreen.
- While answering, **do not switch to other windows, tabs or applications**.
- Keep this page in the foreground. Do not minimize the window or lock the screen.

## Violations and automatic submission

- The system detects actions such as leaving fullscreen, switching windows or tabs, and switching to another application. A countdown appears when you leave: **returning within 10 seconds is not a violation**. It counts as one violation only after 10 seconds.
- **Only one violation is allowed**: the first one shows a warning. Return to fullscreen right away and continue.
- **A second violation submits your quiz automatically** and ends this attempt.
- Once this attempt has ended, whether submitted automatically or by you, **you cannot enter the same attempt again**.

## Academic integrity

Please complete the quiz on your own and respect its fairness. Good luck!

# Quiz Rules

Please read these rules carefully before you begin. Once you select "I have read and agree — start the quiz", the quiz opens in fullscreen.

## Environment requirements

- The screen switches to **fullscreen** when the quiz starts. Please do not leave fullscreen.
- While answering, **do not switch to other windows, tabs or applications**.
- Keep this page in the foreground. Do not minimize the window or lock the screen.

## Record of leaving the quiz

- The system detects actions such as leaving fullscreen, switching windows or tabs, and switching to another application, and shows a warning when you leave.
- This quiz **is not submitted automatically when you leave**. Select return to continue answering.
- However, **the time and length of every absence are recorded**, and your teacher can see them.

## Academic integrity

Please complete the quiz on your own and respect its fairness. Good luck!
